About CarbonTrack

Your personal carbon footprint monitoring tool

What is CarbonTrack?

CarbonTrack helps you monitor and understand your daily carbon emissions. Just like tracking calories for health, CarbonTrack lets you track the environmental impact of your daily activities.

How It Works

  • Create an account - Register to start tracking your emissions
  • Log activities - Record your transportation, energy usage, and food consumption
  • View insights - See detailed breakdowns of your carbon footprint
  • Get recommendations - Receive personalized tips to reduce your impact

What We Track

⚡ Electricity

Fan, AC, lights, heater, fridge, and more

🚗 Transportation

Car, bus, train, flight, bike, and walking

🍽️ Food

Rice, chicken, beef, pork, vegetables, and fruits

🗑️ Waste

Landfill, recycled, and composted waste
